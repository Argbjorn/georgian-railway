title: "Additional Baku - Tbilisi - Baku train now running"
date: 2026-11-02
---
There are now two trains on the Baku - Tbilisi - Baku route, with two departures a day. The additional train was introduced due to high demand.

From Tbilisi: [morning](https://georailway.com/routes/37/), [evening](https://georailway.com/routes/39/). From Baku: [morning](https://georailway.com/routes/40/), [evening](https://georailway.com/routes/38/).
