The KISS is a double-decker electric train built by the Swiss company Stadler Rail. It is the most modern train of Georgian Railway. These trains have been running in Georgia since 2016, and the railway has four of them. They run only on the Tbilisi – Batumi line. Each train has four double-deck cars. There are three classes: second, first and business.

## Amenities

The train has air conditioning. Every car has a toilet on the lower deck. The train is accessible for wheelchair users: there is a lift and an accessible toilet. Luggage space is limited, and in summer suitcases end up in the aisles and by the doors. There are vending machines with water and snacks. Wi-Fi formally exists, but don't count on it: it is very unreliable.

## Classes

### Second class

Takes up three of the four cars. Seats are in a 3+2 layout, simple fabric ones with fold-down tables. Some seats face each other. There are power sockets, but not at every seat.

{{< train-gallery "second" >}}

### First class

In the fourth car. Seats are in a 2+2 layout, and the leather seats are more comfortable than in second class. There are power sockets between the seats.

### Business class

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
There are two kinds of business-class cabins. Tickets cost the same, but the comfort is different. Two trains have a real business class; in the other two it is essentially first class with tables. When buying online, you can tell them apart by the seat map.
{{< /callout >}}

In both cases the cabin is separated from the rest of the car by a solid partition, and from the stairs by a curtain. There is a station with water and hot drinks. Business-class tickets go on sale only a day before departure.

**Real business class.** 2+1 layout, only 9 seats. Comfortable seats with electric adjustment, heating and power sockets. This is the most comfort you can get on Georgian Railway trains.

{{< train-gallery "business" >}}

**First class with tables.** 2+2 layout, 16 seats. The seats are the same as in first class. The only differences are the tables between the rows and more legroom. All seats face each other.
