VMK and GRT are four-car electric trains built by the Chinese company CSR. Georgian Railway has eight of them in total. There is no exact breakdown in open sources, but apparently these are three VMK from 2010, partly assembled in Tbilisi, and five GRT from 2012. Four or five units run regularly, the rest have been retired or stripped for spare parts. The trains run on the routes to Zugdidi, Poti and Ozurgeti. Units are moved between routes, so you can't know in advance which one will come. There are three classes: second, first and business, but business class is not available on every route.

## Amenities

The cars have air conditioning and are mostly clean. There are vending machines with snacks, water and coffee. Power sockets are European type, and there are few of them. No Wi-Fi or USB ports. The toilet is an old-style one.

## Luggage

There are no luggage restrictions. Bags go on the racks above the windows and on the shelf at the end of the car. In first class you can put a bag under the seat.

## Classes

### Second class

Takes up three of the four cars. Seats are in a 2+3 layout.

### First class

In the same car as business class. Seats are in a 2+2 layout and are more comfortable: the backrest reclines and the armrests fold up. There is a large fold-down table, a footrest, a coat hook and a reading lamp. Passengers get a free bottle of water.

### Business class

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
It is essentially the same first class: the seats are the same, there is no service, and the ticket costs almost twice as much.
{{< /callout >}}

The cabin is separated from first class by a curtain, and the seats are in the same 2+2 layout. Trains to Ozurgeti have no business class.
