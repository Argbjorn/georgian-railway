Armenia is a named train of South Caucasus Railway (SCR), which operates the railways in Armenia. The train runs every other day: in winter from Yerevan to Tbilisi, and in summer, from 14 June to 1 October, on to Batumi. The cars were built in 2021 at the Tver Carriage Works. All seats are berths. There are four classes: platzkart, kupe, comfort kupe and SV.

## Amenities

Every car has air conditioning and a toilet with touchless controls, a shower and a baby changing table. There are power sockets and USB ports. Compartments have a safe, a table and a light at every berth, and the upper berths have safety rails. The windows don't open, but there are blackout curtains. Every car has a drinking water cooler that heats water up to 80 °C. The attendant has a fridge and a microwave, and you can heat up your own food there. There is Wi-Fi, but it is slow and unreliable.

## Food

There is no dining car and no food cart. Bring your own food and water.

## Bed linen and luggage

Bed linen and insurance are included in the ticket in all classes. Small luggage goes on the shelf above the compartment door, large luggage under the lower berths. If you have heavy luggage, it is better to take a lower berth.

## Classes

### Platzkart (open-plan sleeper)

An open-plan car with lower and upper berths.

### Kupe (4-berth compartment)

A closed compartment with four berths: two lower and two upper. The compartment locks from the inside.

### Comfort kupe

The same 4-berth compartment. The only difference is the travel kit: tea, coffee, a sandwich and a toiletry kit.

### SV (2-berth compartment)

A compartment for two, both berths are lower ones. The travel kit is the same as in comfort kupe. The compartment has a TV.
