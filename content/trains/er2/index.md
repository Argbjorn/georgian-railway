The ER2 is a Soviet electric train built by the Riga Carriage Works in the 1960s–1970s. Georgian Railway runs three variants:

- **ER2**, not modernized;
- **ER2M**, partly modernized, each unit in its own way;
- **ES**, ER2s rebuilt in Tbilisi since 2007 with a new cab and a refurbished interior.

The condition of the units varies a lot, and you can't know in advance which one will come. It may be a refurbished ES with soft seats or an old ER2 with plastic benches.

## Interior

There is only one class. Modernized units have soft seats in a 2+2 layout, some of them facing each other. Unmodernized ER2s have plastic benches with padded inserts in a 3+2 layout.

## Amenities

There are toilets (usually in poor condition) and heating. There is no air conditioning, so the cars are hot in summer, but the windows open. No power sockets.

## Tickets

Tickets are sold at the station ticket office, seats are not assigned. There is a conductor on board, who only accepts bank cards. The old ticket machines that took coins are gone.
