This is not an electric multiple unit but a VL10 or VL11 electric locomotive hauling one or two passenger coaches. These trains run between Khashuri and Zestafoni. Locals call this train "Masterovoy" (the workers' train), because it is mostly used by railway workers.

{{< train-gallery >}}

## Amenities

The coaches are old Soviet-type seated cars. Seats are in a 2+2 layout and are more comfortable than in electric trains. There is a toilet and heating, no air conditioning. No power sockets.
